Our story.

Nexus started as a personal frustration. Existing trading apps made withdrawing money feel like a punishment — verification mazes, week-long waits, and mysterious fees.

We built the tool we wanted to use ourselves: one app that lets you trade in seconds and withdraw in minutes, on any platform you happen to be using.

Today, Nexus is used by traders and small businesses who need money to move on their schedule, not their bank's.
