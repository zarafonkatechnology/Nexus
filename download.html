Install guides.

Follow the steps for your platform. Most installs take under 2 minutes.

Windows
1 Run the downloaded Nexus-Setup.exe
2 If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC":
Click "More info"
Then click "Run anyway"
3 Follow the setup wizard to install
4 Right-click the taskbar icon → Pin to taskbar
macOS
1 Open the downloaded Nexus.dmg file
2 Drag Nexus into your Applications folder
3 First launch — macOS may block an unsigned app:
In Applications, Control-click the Nexus icon
Choose Open → confirm "Open"
4 Enable Telegram notifications — so alerts come to your phone:
Open Nexus → Settings → Notifications
Choose "Connect with Telegram"
Scan the QR code with the Telegram app on your phone
Confirm the link — alerts now push to your phone via Telegram
Android
1 Open the downloaded Nexus.apk file
2 Allow install from unknown sources:
Tap "Settings" when prompted
Enable "Allow from this source"
3 Tap Install and open Nexus
4 Allow notifications — tap "Allow" when the app asks
5 Disable battery saver restrictions:
Open Settings → Apps → Nexus
Tap "App info"
Go to Battery → Battery saver
Change to "No restrictions"
⚠️ Why disable battery saver? Android aggressively kills background apps to save power — which stops Nexus from delivering notifications on time. Setting Nexus to "No restrictions" keeps alerts instant and reliable.
